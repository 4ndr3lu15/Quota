# Quota

> One small obelisk that tells you if you can still work.

Feeling: discreet, honest, glanceable

## For
For someone who works with AI plans that run out. It is glanced at in the top bar dozens of times a day, and clicked only when the color changes.

## It is
- one mark in the top bar, colored by your pace
- every account's limits, in plain words
- part of the shell, in its own style

## It is not
- a billing or spending dashboard
- an app to live in: its window is for a closer look at usage and logs, then closing
- a source of alerts and noise

## Motion
Almost motionless. The obelisk shifts color quietly as the pace changes, and the menu opens and closes the way the shell's own menus do.
